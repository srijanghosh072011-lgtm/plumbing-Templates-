Drain Cleaning & Unclogging questions

The things customers ask us most about this specific service.

How much does drain cleaning cost in Regina?

Expect $180 to $280 for a single slow fixture such as a kitchen sink or tub, and $320 to $450 for a main sewer line clearing that includes camera inspection. Heavy root intrusion needing hydro-jetting runs higher; we quote that separately once the camera shows what is actually in the line.

Why does my drain keep clogging after it is cleared?

A repeat clog almost always means the blockage was pushed through rather than removed, or there is a structural cause — a belly in the pipe, root intrusion at a joint, or scale buildup narrowing the line. That is why we camera every main line: clearing without looking is how the same clog comes back in six weeks.

Are chemical drain cleaners a bad idea?

For older Regina homes with cast-iron or ABS drains, yes. Caustic cleaners generate heat, can soften ABS joints and accelerate corrosion in cast iron, and they rarely clear a full blockage — they channel a narrow hole through it. Mechanical clearing costs more once but does not damage the pipe.
