Sewer Line Repair questions

The things customers ask us most about this specific service.

How much does sewer line replacement cost?

A localized spot repair where one joint or section has failed runs $900 to $2,400. A full replacement from the house to the property line is $4,500 to $9,000 depending on depth, length and what is on top of it — replacing under a mature driveway or landscaping costs meaningfully more than under open lawn.

What causes sewer lines to fail in Regina?

Three things dominate here: root intrusion at joints on older clay tile, ground movement from our expansive clay soil creating bellies and offsets, and simple age on cast iron that has corroded from the inside. The freeze-thaw cycle accelerates all three, which is why Regina sees more line failures than milder-climate cities.

Can a sewer line be repaired without digging up the yard?

Sometimes. Trenchless pipe lining or pipe bursting works when the existing line is intact enough to host a liner and the route has access pits at both ends. It is not viable if the line has collapsed, has a severe offset, or runs under obstacles that prevent pit placement. The camera survey tells us which is realistic before you commit.
