Before you call

Quick answers

Do you offer 24/7 emergency plumbing service?

Yes. Northline Plumbing answers emergency calls 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including statutory holidays. A live dispatcher picks up rather than an answering service, and the average on-site arrival across Regina is 90 minutes. There is no overtime surcharge for nights, weekends or holidays.

What areas do you serve?

Regina and the surrounding communities within roughly 120 kilometres, including Moose Jaw, White City, Pilot Butte, Emerald Park and Lumsden. If you are outside that radius, call anyway — we will tell you honestly whether we can reach you in a reasonable time or refer you to someone closer.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Northline holds a Saskatchewan Journeyperson Plumber licence, carries $2 million in general liability insurance, is bonded, and all technicians are covered under WCB. We provide licence and insurance documentation on request before work begins.

How much do you charge for a service call?

The diagnostic call-out is $149, and that amount is credited toward the repair if you approve the work the same day. After diagnosis you receive a written flat quote for the repair itself — we do not bill open-ended hourly, so the number you approve is the number you pay.
