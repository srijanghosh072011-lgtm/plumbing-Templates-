REGINA PLUMBING / EVERYDAY PLUMBING

Small problems deserve a proper fix.

A dripping faucet or a toilet that will not stop running can make the whole house feel out of sorts. Start with a careful diagnosis, understand the repair options, and agree on the work before it begins.

What we can plan for.

  • Faucet and fixture repairs
  • Toilet repairs and replacements
  • Accessible water-line leaks
  • Water-pressure troubleshooting

Make the first conversation count.

Tell us which fixture is affected, when you first noticed the issue, and whether it happens constantly or only while water is running. A clear photo can help prepare for the visit.
