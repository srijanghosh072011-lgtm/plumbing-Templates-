HOT WATER / 3 min read

Repair or replace your water heater?

Four useful questions to ask before deciding what comes next for your hot water.

Copper & Prairie · Homeowner journal · September 4, 2026

Begin with the symptom

No hot water, unusual sounds, visible water, and an inconsistent temperature are different problems. Note exactly what changed and when. A symptom on its own is not a reliable diagnosis, and an inspection is a better starting point than ordering a replacement.

Know what is already installed

A photo of the unit label can identify the model and capacity. Gather the approximate installation date and any service records you have. Keep clear of hot surfaces and do not remove equipment covers to find information.

Compare complete options

Ask for the repair scope and the replacement scope in writing. A useful comparison includes equipment, labour, disposal, venting or connection work, and any required upgrades. A headline equipment price is not the same as a finished installation price.

Think about the household

The right choice depends partly on how the home uses hot water. A growing household, new bathroom, or different daily schedule can change demand. Ask about suitable capacity and the practical requirements of a tankless system rather than choosing on trend alone.
