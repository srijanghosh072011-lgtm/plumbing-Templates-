Common questions

Straight answers.

The questions we get asked before anyone books. See all.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Coldsnap Plumbing & Heating holds Saskatchewan journeyman plumber licence SK-JP-118472, carries Prairie Mutual — $5,000,000 general liability, and maintains WCB coverage under account WCB 0442891. We will show you all three on request, and you should ask any contractor for them before work starts.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes, genuinely 24/7 — a person answers, not a voicemail box. After-hours call-out is a flat rate quoted on the phone before we dispatch, and it is credited against the repair if you approve the work.

How do you price work?

Flat rate, quoted in writing before we start. You approve a number, not an hourly meter, so a job that takes longer than expected is our problem rather than yours. If we open something up and find a genuinely different job underneath, we stop and re-quote before continuing.

What areas do you serve?

Regina and roughly 60 km around it — including White City, Pilot Butte, Lumsden, Balgonie. If you are outside that, call anyway; we will tell you honestly whether we can get there in a useful timeframe.
